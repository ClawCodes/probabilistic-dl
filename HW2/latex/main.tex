\begin{abstract}

\section{Question and experimental design}
Explain why predictive uncertainty matters. State the task, 2-D synthetic sanity check, realistic high-dimensional dataset (recommended: Fashion-MNIST), train/test splits, preprocessing, and fixed seeds. Define train/test accuracy or error, predictive entropy, expected data uncertainty, and mutual information. State how hyperparameters were selected without using the test set.

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Standard deep ensembles}
Describe independently initialized models trained on identical data with distinct data-order seeds. Give the averaged predictive distribution $p(y\mid x,\mathcal D) \approx M^{-1}\sum_m p_m(y\mid x)$. State architecture, optimizer, learning rate, epochs, and ensemble sizes. Explain the L2 loss term and its isotropic zero-mean Gaussian-prior interpretation; report its coefficient.

\subsection{Comparison method: MC dropout}
When implemented, describe dropout placement/rate, number of stochastic passes, training objective, and retaining dropout at test time. Explain approximate Bayesian sampling and keep setup comparable to the ensemble.

\section{Results on the 2-D synthetic task}
Show decision probabilities and uncertainty surfaces. Verify uncertainty behavior away from observations and at ambiguous boundaries; discuss failures. Compare train/test performance. Vary $M\in\{1,2,5,10\}$ and interpret accuracy/error, predictive entropy, and mutual information.

\section{Realistic high-dimensional dataset}
Report the same train/test metrics and uncertainty summaries on Fashion-MNIST (or justify a replacement). Include a table comparing standard deep ensembles and MC dropout at matched compute and an ensemble-size plot. Analyze uncertain correct and incorrect examples and whether uncertainty identifies likely mistakes. Do not claim calibration from a single mean uncertainty number.

\section{Comparison and discussion}
Directly compare methods on accuracy/error, uncertainty behavior, compute/memory, and sensitivity to ensemble size or MC sample count. Explain limitations: finite seeds, dataset scope, hyperparameter sensitivity, and the difference between in-distribution ambiguity and out-of-distribution uncertainty. Mention unexpected results and plausible explanations.

\section{Conclusion}
Give two or three concise takeaways about which method is more useful for each goal, backed by the results.
